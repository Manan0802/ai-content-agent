# तीसरा हाथ — standalone (no parts)

A complete story in one video. Nothing to continue, nothing to wait for — the whole point is that
it resolves, so it gets shared rather than followed.

**Domain:** डर / horror-suspense (new — the dhaba serial is drama)
**Language:** Haryanvi
**Target:** 5 clips, ~30s after trimming the dead tails

## Why horror for the standalone

A single video has to open, turn and land inside 30 seconds. Horror is the one popular domain
built that way — the whole form is setup, turn, punch. It also plays to what we can actually do:
night and fog hide the character drift that daylight exposes, and the story needs only two people
in one location.

## Cast lock — repeat verbatim in every prompt

- **राजू (rider):** `a lean young Haryanvi man in his early twenties, short cropped hair, wearing a grey hoodie`
- **सोनू (pillion):** `a stocky young Haryanvi man, wearing a black jacket`
- **Set:** `on an empty village road at night, thick fog, a single motorcycle headlight, cold blue moonlight, 35mm lens`

One speaker per clip — two faces in frame and Veo puts the words on the wrong one.

## End card

`थमनै कदे रात नै वा सड़क देखी सै?`

A question, not a caption. The comments are the distribution.

## Pronunciation care

Two words already burned us: edge-tts read sentence-initial **चाय** as "चाहे", and Veo read
**पहचाण** as "पैर पैन". Nothing here starts on a hard-to-read word, and every line gets the same
Whisper round-trip before it goes anywhere near the cut.
